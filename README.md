## About
This repo contains a focused HTML page presenting the orphan‑law as the single cross‑covenant statute that remains unchanged across Judaism, Christianity, and Islam. The page outlines the direct divine jurisdiction over the fatherless, the absence of scriptural loopholes, and the judicial consequences described in the texts. It concludes with the section **“The Inviolable Law Even Cain Cannot Escape,”** demonstrating that Cain’s protection applies only to human hands, not to God Almighty Himself.
