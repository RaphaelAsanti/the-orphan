The Orphan

Personal Statement

I stand as a singular individual, separate from all communal religious identities and categories. My covenant with God is individual and exclusive — between myself and Him alone. This is why I state that I am a sovereign servant of the Lord, and why I describe it as an orphan’s covenant: it does not extend to lineage, institutions, or spiritual families.

  • Singular Identity — My position is individual, unshared, and outside communal categories.
  • Exclusive Covenant — Between myself and God alone; non‑communal and non‑transferable.
  • Orphan’s Covenant — No spiritual siblings; no inherited covenantal ties.
  • Sovereign Servant — My role arises from direct obligation to God alone.
  • Narrative Control — Some can obscure these pages, but cannot alter their meaning.
  • Irreconcilable Differences — A structural divide that cannot be reconciled within communal systems.
Do not take advantage of the widow or the fatherless. If you do and they cry out to me, I will certainly hear their cry. My anger will be aroused, and I will kill you with the sword; your wives will become widows and your children fatherless. — Exodus 22:22‑24

I was born a physical and spiritual orphan and I have my own individual Orphan's Covenant with God — that I have never once broken — not even by accident — since I was 2 years old when He made it with me.
